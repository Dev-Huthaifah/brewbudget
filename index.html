A CLEAR CUP, A CLEAR CALCULATION

How the estimate works

We multiply your café cups per day by the price per cup and the number of days each week, then estimate 52 weeks. The home-brew comparison uses the same number of cups and the home cost you set above.

Weekly café spendcups × café price × daysYearly home-brew costcups × home cost × days × 52

This is an estimate, not a promise of savings. It excludes equipment purchases, milk, taxes, travel, waste, and changes in your routine. Enter your own home-brew cost to make the comparison more useful.
